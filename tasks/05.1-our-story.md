## Video

- Pick a free cupcake-decorating video on **pixabay.com** (Pixabay Content License), close in mood to the Figma placeholder. Record the URL and author in `CREDITS.md`.
- Download the smallest quality that stays sharp at 1220px wide (usually 1280×720); target **≤ 8 MB**, ≤ 30 s. Re-encode with ffmpeg if larger (H.264, `-crf 28`, no audio track if it has music you don't need).
- Put it in `public/media/our-story.mp4`.
- `<video controls preload="metadata" playsinline>` with **no Figma poster**: the preview is the video's first frame. Use `src="/media/our-story.mp4#t=0.1"` so Safari also paints the first frame.
- No autoplay. Rounded corners and size as in Figma; keeps a 16:9 ratio (`aspect-ratio`) at every width.

## Map

Facade pattern (ADR-5): static image first, Google Maps iframe only after a click.

- Place: **Pego** (Alicante, Spain), from the link https://share.google/Qho2IaQqiXrVBRdqa. Open the link, take the exact place name and coordinates, and keep them in `src/data/store.ts` (name, address, lat/lng, Google Maps URL); everything below reads from there.
- **Static image:** export the area around the store from openstreetmap.org (Share → Image, zoom so streets are readable), convert to WebP ≤ 200 KB, save as `src/assets/images/our-story/map.webp`. Draw the pin in CSS/SVG at the store's position (not baked into the image) in brand colours. Show "© OpenStreetMap contributors" in a corner of the image, linked to openstreetmap.org/copyright.
- **Button** over the image: "Show interactive map" (`Button`, `type="button"`). On click, render `<iframe src="https://www.google.com/maps?q=<lat>,<lng>&z=15&output=embed">` in the same box, then move focus to the iframe.
- Iframe: `title="Miss Cupcake store location"`, `referrerpolicy="no-referrer-when-downgrade"`, no border, `allowfullscreen`.
- Image and iframe share one container: rounded corners and size as in Figma, `aspect-ratio` instead of a fixed height, so nothing shifts when the iframe replaces the image.
- Always-visible text link "Open in Google Maps" (new tab, `rel="noopener noreferrer"`) under the map.
- Component: `src/components/MapFacade/` (props: image, alt, embed URL, link URL), so it can be reused.
