# Task 6.4: Accessibility check

Audit every route against **WCAG 2.2 AA** and fix what fails. Do after 6.0–6.3.

Routes: `/`, `/` with the newsletter banner, `/our-story` (before and after "Show interactive map"), `/why-miss-cupcake`, My Bag open (empty and full), `/checkout` (empty and full), `/checkout/payment` (empty form, after empty submit), Confirmed dialog, 404.

## Checks

1. **Automated:** axe-core (DevTools MCP / axe extension, or `@axe-core/cli` against `yarn preview`) on every state above at 375 and 1440.
2. **Keyboard:** full order flow with keyboard only; logical Tab order; no traps (except the modal dialog); skip link "Skip to main content" as the first focusable element (add it: not in Figma, visually hidden until focused).
3. **Screen reader:** VoiceOver (macOS) or NVDA if available, otherwise the DevTools Accessibility tree: landmarks (`header`, `nav`, `main`, `footer`), heading outline per page (one `h1`, no skipped levels), names of icon buttons, live regions (cart "Added", totals, banner, form error count).
4. **Zoom and reflow:** 200% zoom at 1440, and 320px width (WCAG 1.4.10): no content lost, no horizontal scroll; text spacing override (WCAG 1.4.12) doesn't clip text.
5. **Contrast:** text on every band and image (cream text on purple, purple on pink/mint/yellow, button labels, placeholders, OSM credit on the map) ≥ 4.5:1, large text ≥ 3:1. List each pair with its ratio.
6. **Motion and media:** `prefers-reduced-motion` respected; video has no autoplay; decorative images `alt=""`.
7. **Page titles:** each route sets a unique `document.title` ("Our story – Miss Cupcake"); `lang="en"` on `<html>`.
8. **Target size:** interactive targets ≥ 24×24px (WCAG 2.5.8), including stepper −/+, ×, OSM credit.

## 2. Keyboard

Real key presses (Chrome, headed), 375 and 1440, the whole flow with the keyboard only: first Tab = "Skip to main content"; Enter moves focus into `<main>` and the next Tab is "SHOP NOW"; Tab to "add to cart", Enter (badge "Cart, 1 item"); Shift+Tab to the cart button, Enter opens My Bag (focus on "Close My Bag"), Tab to "Check out", Enter; on `/checkout` Tab to the payment link, Enter (`/checkout/payment`, title "Payment – Miss Cupcake"); Tab to the delivery radios, ArrowDown selects Birthday; typing into all 14 fields by Tab order; Enter on "Place order" opens the dialog with focus on "Done", title "Order confirmed – Miss Cupcake"; Tab stays in the dialog (the one Tab that leaves it goes to the browser UI, as for any native modal); Enter on "Done" returns to `/`, cart `[]`, session storage empty. Same result at both widths. **My Bag open, then Tab out** (found in code review): the bag used to stay open on top of `<main>`, so the next focused control ("SHOP NOW") could sit under the card (WCAG 2.4.11). `SiteHeader` now closes the bag when focus moves anywhere outside the bag and the cart button (`focusin`), which also covers the skip link. Checked with real keys at 375 and 960: Close -> Check out -> View cart stay open, the next Tab ("SHOP NOW") closes it; Shift+Tab to the skip link and Enter closes it and focuses `<main>`.

Tab traversal of every page (no trap; the focus leaves the document at the end): home 47 / 49 stops (375 / 1440), our story 20 / 22, why 13 / 15, check out 16 / 18, payment 28 / 31, 404 4 / 6. Every stop has a 2px solid ring (video and the OSM iframe draw their own). With the interactive map the Tab cycle also completes (video -> iframe -> Google Maps link -> footer -> out). The ring of the skip link sits on the purple notice bar, where a purple ring would not show; the link is a cream tab with a purple border, which does (`tasks/screenshots/06.4-skip-link-1440.png`, `-375.png`).

## Deviations and limits

1. **OpenStreetMap iframe** (`frame-tested`): cross-origin third-party content, cannot be audited from here. The iframe has a title, a visible credit link and the "Open in Google Maps" link as an alternative; it is one tab stop and does not trap focus.
2. **Our Story video** (`video-caption`): it has no audio track (`ffprobe`: video stream only), so there is nothing to caption; its alternative is the `aria-label` "Cupcake turning on a stand". A fuller description would be a copy decision.
3. **No VoiceOver / NVDA run**: the accessibility tree, descriptions and live-region DOM were checked, but speech output was not heard.
4. **Route changes through React Router links** (check out -> payment, back links) update the title but do not move focus or announce the new page; header navigation is full page loads, which do. Not a failure of the listed checks; noted for a later pass.
5. **Placeholders look like values** (they inherit the text color at full opacity, as drawn in Figma). The contrast passes and every field has a visible label, but a user may take "Josh" for typed text. Not changed: it is the design.
6. **The static `<title>` in `index.html` is gone**: before React mounts the tab shows the URL for a moment.
7. **Sticker overlap on the Why page heading** (see section 5): design, contrast of the sticker pixels not measured.
8. **Skip link on the purple notice bar**: its purple focus ring would be invisible there, so the link is a cream tab with its own purple border instead (see 2).
